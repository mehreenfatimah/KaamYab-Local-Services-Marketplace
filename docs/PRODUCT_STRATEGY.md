# KaamYab product strategy

## Positioning

KaamYab is a two-sided local-services marketplace designed for practical work that still happens mostly through phone calls, personal references and informal networks.

The product thesis is:

> **Make local skilled work easier to request, easier to compare and easier to access.**

## Product principles

1. **Problem first** — customers can describe what is wrong instead of needing to know the exact service category.
2. **Choice, not forced matching** — customers compare nearby offers by price, availability and reputation.
3. **Low-friction access** — text, tap-based controls and short voice input all lead to the same request flow.
4. **Trust through work history** — profiles emphasize completed jobs, ratings, availability and verification signals.
5. **Worker control** — workers choose when to go online and can accept a budget or send another offer.

## Voice

Voice is an accessibility feature, not the product's only interface.

Customer voice flow:

1. record one short description;
2. transcribe it;
3. infer the likely service when possible;
4. show the structured request back to the customer;
5. let the customer confirm or edit before posting.

Worker onboarding uses simple explicit fields rather than trying to extract an entire profile from one long recording. A short voice introduction can be added later as optional profile media.

## Launch focus

A marketplace needs density rather than nationwide breadth.

Recommended first-market shape:

- geography: Rawalpindi + Islamabad;
- categories: electrician, plumber, AC technician, carpenter, painter and cleaning/repair;
- customer promise: useful nearby choices with visible availability;
- worker promise: simple access to local jobs and a growing work reputation.

## Prototype boundaries

The repository uses synthetic workers, offers, jobs, ratings and earnings. It does not claim real identity verification, live dispatch, production payments or real completed-job history.
